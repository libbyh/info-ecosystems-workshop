About the Workshop

Connecting the micro and the macro

Researchers often study a single place people access information — a social media platform, a community, a health topic — in isolation. This workshop aims to move beyond those isolated studies by fostering a systemic, holistic conversation across all levels of the information ecosystem. We welcome researchers who wish to understand how individual behaviours and environmental factors influence one another: those studying information seeking and retrieval, algorithmic curation, narrative diffusion, media literacy, and the spread of disinformation using computational, behavioural, or humanistic methods.

An ecosystem is not merely a collection of individuals, and individuals do not act in a vacuum. The various levels of behaviour co-constitute one another, and we can understand their impacts only by studying them in an explicitly multi-scale way. Our primary goal is to establish a collaborative research agenda. Participants will work together to define at least one concrete research project and team, and we will draft a white paper based on the workshop that outlines a roadmap for this broader multi-scale research program.

What participants will leave with

  1. A shared map of questions about the information environment that require collaboration across scales, methods, or traditions to answer.
  2. A collaborative research plan — at least one group leaves with the outline of a research project they intend to start.
  3. Connection to a continuing interest group and a white paper in progress.

Program

What we’ll do

01

Collect Assumptions

Surface the working assumptions of your scholarly community and find where they conflict, complement, or open new research directions when placed alongside others.

02

Brainstorm

In small groups, ask: what would you study if you could resolve one of those tensions? What question requires your collaborator’s scale, method, or data to be answerable at all?

03

Map Affinities

Cluster ideas, vote with dot stickers, and form groups around shared research interests to anchor the final planning activity.

04

Who, What, When

Draft a concrete research project outline — methods, data, leads, and timelines — and present a one-minute summary to the full group.

Wrap-up: Individual commitments + shared next steps across three areas: white paper writing, coordinating an ongoing interest group, and driving the outlined research projects forward.

Who Should Come

Researchers who look up and down the stack

We invite scholars who want to understand how individual behaviours and system-level forces shape one another — whether you approach the problem computationally, behaviourally, or through humanistic inquiry. If you have ever felt your findings stopped at the edge of your method or scale, this workshop is for you.
